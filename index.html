Frequently asked questions

Can I pay when my order arrives?

Request Pay on Delivery during checkout. James will confirm whether it is available for your delivery area and any applicable conditions.

Is my order placed automatically?

No. The checkout prepares a WhatsApp order enquiry. The seller must confirm the order, stock and final amount.

How do I choose my size?

Select a preferred size on each item and ask James for exact garment measurements before confirming.

Can I return an item?

Ask James for the applicable return and exchange terms before purchasing. This demo does not publish an unverified returns policy.
